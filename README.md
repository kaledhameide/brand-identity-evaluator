# 👗 Brand Identity Evaluator (Fashion Branding AI App)

A classroom-friendly **Streamlit app** that evaluates the effectiveness of fashion brand identities using **color and font psychology**.  
Developed for students in **Fashion Design and Merchandising** to explore how visual branding communicates positioning and target audience.

---

## 🎯 Purpose
This app helps students analyze how well a brand’s logo and visual identity align with its intended message and market.  
By combining design psychology and branding strategy, it generates an **Effectiveness Score (0–100)** and gives clear feedback.

Students can:
- Enter a brand’s **positioning statement**, **segment**, and **target audience**  
- Input the **dominant logo colors** and **font style**  
- Instantly receive a **score, explanation, and improvement suggestions**

No coding required — just open the web link and start exploring.
